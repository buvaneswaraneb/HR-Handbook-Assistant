# Osmium Landing Page Features

Use this document as the product copy source for the Osmium landing or welcome page. The language should stay friendly, clear, and non-technical.

## Hero

### Headline

AI focused employee management workspace.

### Supporting Copy

Osmium helps teams keep employee records, team structure, projects, leave, and documents in one clear place. With AI-assisted HR knowledge, managers can quickly understand availability, absences, responsibilities, and project progress without switching between tools.

### Short Value Line

Manage people, projects, leave, documents, and HR knowledge from one calm workspace.

## Core Feature Cards

### Private Workspace

A trusted place for employee records, leave details, team documents, and workplace updates.

Use this feature to show that Osmium keeps sensitive HR workflows in one dedicated team space without making the landing page sound technical.

### Organized Teams and Projects

See teams, roles, project ownership, deadlines, and responsibilities from one clean workspace.

Use this feature to explain that Osmium connects people with the work they own, so managers can understand who is doing what and where attention is needed.

### Helpful AI Assistant

Ask about availability, absences, skills, documents, and project updates in plain language.

Use this feature to present the assistant as a practical workplace helper, not a technical chatbot. It should feel useful for day-to-day HR and project questions.

## Expanded Feature Sections

### Employee Management

Keep profiles, roles, contact details, teams, skills, and work status together. Osmium gives managers a simple way to view employee information and understand who is available for upcoming work.

Suggested landing copy:

> Know your team at a glance. View employee details, skills, roles, and status without digging through scattered files.

### Leave and Availability

Track who is available, who is away, and which absences may affect project planning. This helps teams make better decisions before assigning work or setting deadlines.

Suggested landing copy:

> Plan around real availability. See leave, absences, and team capacity before work gets assigned.

### Project Visibility

Connect employees to projects, deadlines, owners, and priorities. Osmium helps teams spot critical work, overdue items, and ownership gaps faster.

Suggested landing copy:

> Keep projects connected to people. Track owners, deadlines, priorities, and progress in the same place as your team data.

### Documents and Files

Keep HR documents and project files close to the people and work they belong to. Teams can use documents as part of their workspace knowledge instead of leaving them isolated.

Suggested landing copy:

> Bring documents into the workflow. Keep files close to employees, projects, and the questions your team needs answered.

### AI-Assisted HR Knowledge

Use plain-language questions to find useful answers from employee, project, leave, and document information. The assistant should support managers by summarizing and surfacing relevant details.

Suggested landing copy:

> Ask the workspace. Get quick answers about availability, absences, skills, projects, and documents in everyday language.

### Dashboard Overview

Show the most important team and project signals in one place: active employees, project status, leave visibility, recent activity, and priority work.

Suggested landing copy:

> Start with the right signals. See team health, project movement, absences, and priorities from a single dashboard.

### Org Tree and Canvas

Explore team structure visually with org tree and canvas views. These views help managers understand relationships, reporting lines, and how teams are arranged.

Suggested landing copy:

> Understand the shape of your team. Explore people, teams, and reporting lines with visual workspace views.

## Landing Page Structure

1. Hero section with headline, supporting copy, and sign-in action.
2. Three feature cards: Private, Organized, Helpful.
3. Employee management section.
4. Leave and availability section.
5. Project visibility section.
6. AI assistant section.
7. Documents and dashboard section.
8. Final call to action: sign in or continue to workspace.

## Voice Guidelines

Use words like team, workspace, availability, absences, projects, documents, and answers.

Avoid technical terms like JWT, RAG, injected context, API, token, backend, endpoint, or database on the landing page.

Keep copy direct and practical. Osmium should feel like a calm workplace tool for managers and HR teams.
