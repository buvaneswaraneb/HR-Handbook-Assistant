# Workspace and Sign-In

Osmium starts with a focused welcome screen and a sign-in panel. The goal is to make the product feel private, calm, and easy to enter without exposing technical details to the user.

## Welcome Screen

The welcome screen introduces Osmium as an AI focused employee management workspace. It explains that teams can manage employee records, team structure, projects, leave, documents, and AI-assisted HR knowledge from one place.

## Sign-In Options

Users can sign in with:

- Google
- Employee email
- Password when the account requires it

The sign-in screen uses friendly language such as "Enter your email to continue" and "New teammates can join after approval."

## User Profile Area

After sign-in, the top bar shows:

- User avatar
- User name
- User email
- Logout action

This helps users understand which workspace session they are using.

## Main Navigation

The left sidebar organizes the product into clear areas:

- Workspace: Dashboard, Canvas, Org Tree
- Manage: Employees, Projects, Files
- Tools: AI Assistant, Leave
- Bottom controls: Settings and Help

## Top Bar Actions

The top bar gives users fast access to common work:

- Global search for employees, skills, and teams
- Quick add employee
- Quick add project
- Mini AI assistant
- Settings

## Global Search

The global search is designed for quick employee discovery. Users can search by skill, team, or related terms and jump into employee results without opening filters first.

## Quick Actions

Osmium supports quick entry points from both the dashboard and top bar:

- Add Employee
- New Project
- Open Canvas
- AI Assistant

## Protected Workspace Experience

The app should describe access in user-friendly terms such as "Private", "Team workspace", and "trusted place for employee records." Avoid technical terms in user-facing copy.
